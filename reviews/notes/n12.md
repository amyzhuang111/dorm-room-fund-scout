## Self-critique
- 3D orbit reads as a founder cloud; fog and island discs keep white breathing room closer to Nature than flat haze alone.
- Timeline makes cohort structure visible; most founders still have no year, so the "left out" label is honest and the play animation is sparse until 2025-2026.
- Hub labels near Penn no longer stack after layout repulsion + DOM collision; they still drift from true 3D projection because overlays use a fit plane for gate stability.
- Mid bloom tracking boxes live mainly in the 2D fallback; WebGL mid tier relies on stronger island glow and edge opacity instead.
- Bundle stays lazy (dynamic import of three); preserveDrawingBuffer is on for visibility sampling only.
